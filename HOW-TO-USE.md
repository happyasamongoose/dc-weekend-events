# DC Weekends — how to use it

**Open it here:** https://happyasamongoose.github.io/dc-weekend-events/

That's it. No login, no app to install, nothing to pay for. It works on a phone,
a tablet or a laptop.

> **Tip:** on a phone, open that link and add it to your home screen — iPhone:
> Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Android: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*. Then it opens
> like an app.

---

## The three rows at the top

Everything on the page is controlled by the three rows under the title. They stay
put while you scroll, so you can change your mind at any point.

### 1. Which weekend?

The first row is a set of weekend buttons. The one on the left says **This
Weekend**; the rest are labelled by date, like **Sep 4–6**.

A "weekend" here means **Friday, Saturday and Sunday** — Friday night counts.

Tap a different weekend and every card below changes to what's on then. You can
look about six weekends ahead. The far-out ones have fewer things listed so far
(the page tells you so) — more get added as those dates get closer.

### 2. What kind of thing?

The second row picks the *type* of thing you feel like doing:

**All · Free & Low-Cost · Outdoor · Music & Concerts · Theater & Performances ·
Comedy & Standup · Museums & Culture · Arts & Galleries · Sports & Active ·
Biking & Trails · Food & Markets · Family & Teens**

Two of those work a bit differently:

- **Free & Low-Cost** shows anything free or cheap, whatever type it is.
- **Family & Teens** shows anything suitable and actually interesting if you're
  roughly 11–16 — again, whatever type it is.

At the end of the row is **★ Saved**, which is your own shortlist. More on that
below.

### 3. Where?

The third row is neighborhoods. Four are on when you arrive:

**Capitol Hill · Southwest / The Wharf · Navy Yard / Ballpark · Downtown /
National Mall**

Tap any of them to turn it off or back on. A dot appears next to the ones that
are on.

Two extra buttons:

- **All DC** — adds the rest of the District: U Street, H Street NE and
  everywhere else in DC. Turn this on if the page feels empty, or if you're
  hunting for music (a lot of the venues are on U Street).
- **Worth the Trip** — events in Arlington and Maryland. It's **off** by default,
  because they need a proper journey. Turn it on if you don't mind traveling.

## What's on a card

Each event is one card. From the top:

- A **colored tag** saying what type it is — Music, Theater, Outdoor and so on.
- **Badges**, when they apply:
  - 🟢 **Free** — costs nothing to get in.
  - 🟡 **Low-cost** — roughly $25 or less, or it has pay-what-you-can, rush or
    under-30 tickets.
  - 🔵 **Teens OK** — good for your age group.
  - 🔴 **18+** or **21+** — *adults only.* Most comedy clubs and some bars are
    like this. You won't get in, so don't plan around it.
- The **name** of the event.
- **When** it is:
  - `Sat, Sep 5` — a single day.
  - `Now through Sep 6` — already running, ends on that date.
  - `Sep 5–6` — hasn't started yet; those are the dates it runs.
  - `Every weekend` — an always-on place like Eastern Market or a park. These sit
    at the bottom under **Always on weekends**.
- **Where** — the venue and its neighborhood.
- A **short description**, and the **price** at the bottom left.

## The three buttons on every card

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **☆** | Saves the event to your shortlist. Tap again to unsave. |
| **+ CAL** | Opens Google Calendar with the event already filled in. Check the details, then press Save. It's added as an all-day event — the actual start time is written in the notes, because venues describe times in all sorts of ways. |
| **↗ INFO** | Opens the venue's own website in a new tab. **Always check this before you go** — that's where the real times, prices and any "sold out" news live. |

## Your shortlist (★ Saved)

Tap **☆** on anything you like the look of. It turns gold. Then tap the
**★ Saved** tab to see just those.

Things worth knowing:

- Your stars are saved **on the device you're using**. Your list on your phone
  isn't the same as someone else's list on theirs — everyone builds their own,
  and nobody else can see yours.
- The **Saved** tab ignores the weekend and neighborhood filters on purpose. It
  shows you everything you've starred, so you can use it as your running wish
  list.
- Clearing your browser data will clear your stars too.
- If a saved event disappears, it's usually because it has already happened.

## Good to know

**The list refreshes every Thursday.** New events appear, past ones drop off. If
you starred something, it stays starred.

**Double-check before you leave the house.** Every listing was real when it was
found, but venues cancel things, sell out and change prices. Tap **↗ INFO** and
look at the venue's own page. If the price on the card and the price on the
venue's site disagree, the venue is right.

**Ask a grown-up before you go anywhere**, especially anything outside the
neighborhoods you know, or anything on **Worth the Trip**.

**If the page looks empty**, you've probably filtered too hard. In order:

1. Turn on **All DC**.
2. Go back to the **All** tab.
3. Try a different weekend.
4. Turn on **Worth the Trip**.

## If something looks wrong

**A gold banner at the top saying "Listings last updated … — the weekly refresh
may have failed."**
The automatic update didn't run. What you're seeing is old but real. Tell whoever
looks after the site.

**"Sample data — test fixture, not live listings."**
You've got `?data=sample` on the end of the address. Those events are made up for
testing. Remove that bit of the address, or just open the plain link at the top
of this page.

**"Live listings not published yet — showing always-on spots."**
The site is up but has no event list yet, so it's showing the places that are
open every weekend anyway. Not broken, just early.

**"Couldn't load events data."**
Usually your connection. Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, say so.
